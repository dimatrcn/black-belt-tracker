PENALTY SYSTEM
DAY OUTCOMEEFFECT
✅ Pillar completed+1 progress day. Active streak continues.
⚠️ Missed 1 daySoft penalty. Progress rolls back to your last earned checkpoint (nearest stripe or belt base at or below your current days).
❌ Missed 2+ days in a rowHard penalty. Progress resets all the way down to your permanent FLOOR (the base of the highest belt you've ever locked in).
❄️ Frozen dayNot a miss. Excluded entirely from streak and penalty calculations.
FLOOR LOCK
Once a pillar's progress reaches a belt's base day count (0d / 30d / 90d / 180d / 365d), that belt is locked in permanently as your FLOOR.
No matter how many days you miss afterward, a hard penalty can never drop you below your FLOOR — only stripes and belts above it are at risk.
Stripes earned between belt bases are NOT locked — missing days can roll you back past a stripe down to your FLOOR if the miss streak is long enough.
FREEZES
Each calendar month grants 3 FREEZES.
Use a freeze on a day (past or present) to pause tracking — that day counts toward nothing and breaks nothing.
Unused freezes do not roll over to the next month.
